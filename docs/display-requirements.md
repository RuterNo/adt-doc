# Display Requirements

## Introduction

The following list of requirements is intended for the PTO to adhere to when new vehicles are produced. These are general 
requirements and must be followed in addition to those outlined in the specific _Materialbeskrivelse_.

In the event of a conflict between the requirements in the _Materialbeskrivelse_ and the general requirements listed below, 
the _Materialbeskrivelse_ requirements take precedence.

## General requirements

| **Requirement**                 | **Specification**                                                   |
|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Automatic brightness adjustment | The screen shall adjust brightness based on ambient light.          |
| Non-reflective                  | The screen shall be non-reflective.                                 |
| Viewing angle                   | 178 degrees horizontally and vertically.                            |
| Minimum resolution              | 1920 pixels in width (landscape), 1920 pixels in height (portrait). |
| Minimum available RAM           | 4 GB per screen.                                                    |
